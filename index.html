Holodeck · Recreational Program

Phaser Range

A circular stage in a dark void. Light-targets drift through the black — pop them. A training remote orbits you: when its emitter glows and chimes, a stun bolt follows — step aside or duck, or shoot the bolt out of the air. The instant it fires, the remote flares white: tag it then for the big score. You can hit it any time to make it flinch — counter-fire during the glow breaks its attack. The whole armory waits on the lobby table — two pistols, two rifles, a bat'leth that bats its bolts straight back, and six d'k tahgs for the sash. Grip takes; let go at a holster to stow, let go anywhere else and it drops. Three hits ends the program.

Voyager's court, walls lit in holodeck gold. An eight-inch disc runs the walls, ricocheting off every surface — and when it has run them long enough, it comes looking for you. Shoot it and it ricochets off your beam — angle in, angle out — and comes back faster every time. Take it in the head or the chest and that is a full impact: one of your three pips. There is no remote here and nothing else to shoot — just you, the disc, and the walls.

Worf's program: ruins in the dark, and a Klingon warrior coming for you with a bat'leth. Klingon armor and Klingon weapons only — the bat'leth, the d'k tahg, the disruptor — though a human may wear them. It circles, it winds up, it swings: duck the sweep, step out of the overhead cut, or meet the blade with yours to parry. After every swing it is open — three hits fell it, and the next one comes faster. Three hits on you ends the program.

The trigger charges the phaser out of its cell; let go and every stored joule flies as one bolt. Snap shots get aim assist — dial it in Settings
The power dial
How long it keeps charging: half a second at stun, about a second mid-dial, and at kill it drinks until the cell is dry — one enormous shot
Light-targets
They hold a charge, shrink as you burn it off, and bolt when your muzzle crosses them
Velocity
A bolt bounces the disc and dumps its charge into it — a full cell sends it screaming. Overfeed one and it divides. Take it in the head or chest and it costs a pip
Keep holding
Stay on past a full charge and it vents as a sustained beam instead — no assist, just its own width
Thumb key
B / Y fires as well — the way the swept TNG shell is meant to be worked
Grip
Hold it to hold it. Squeeze near anything — on the table, in a holster, on the sash, on the floor — to take it. Let go at a holster or sash sheath and it stows; let go anywhere else and it drops (over the table, onto the table). A second hand on a rifle or the bat'leth steadies it
A / X
Hold 0.6 s to pause; a fresh press on the pause card walks you back to the lobby
The lobby
The arch and the table are one room. Three pads on the table's front edge: Phaser Range, Velocity, Begin — trigger with a hand over one. Whatever is on your body comes with you
Thumbstick
Flick right to raise the power setting, left to lower it — ten detents, stun to kill, the lamp runs green to red (desktop: mouse wheel)
Your body
Dodge stun bolts physically — sidestep, duck
Holsters
Two on the hips, two over the shoulders — any weapon fits. The bat'leth wants the left shoulder; either hand draws it, both hands span it. It deflects stun bolts even held still; a returned bolt that hits the remote pays 200
The sash
A Klingon baldric over the right shoulder, six sheaths down the chest, d'k tahgs only. Let a knife go against your chest and it seats in the nearest empty sheath
Throw a blade
Let go mid-swing and it flies. It plants only point-first, at speed — land it flat and it bounces, skids and lies where it stops, like anything else you drop. Heavier weapons throw shorter and tumble slower. Grip near anything lying about to pick it up (desktop: G throws, F recovers)
Calisthenics
Worf's program. BEGIN arms you: a d'k tahg on each hip, the bat'leth over the left shoulder, no sash. Two worlds in Settings — the arena at high noon, run out to the horizon, or Qo'noS at night with the fight lit by lava. A Klingon warrior with a bat'leth: duck the sweep, sidestep the overhead cut, or meet its blade with yours to parry. After a swing it is open — three hits fell it. Klingon armor and weapons only; a hit in its wind-up breaks the attack
Catching
Toss a blade and squeeze as it comes back — it is caught exactly as it lands in your hand, no snap. Re-seat it by holstering and drawing. With a blade in hand the thumbstick trims how it sits: forward/back pitches it toward the knuckles or back, left/right twists it about its length, 10° a flick, remembered
Two hands on a pistol
Grip near a pistol your other hand holds and the second hand steadies it; let go with the first and it passes across
The drones
Tag the remote in its window three times and it bursts; the next one comes in from the dark a little tougher. Five strikes burst the Velocity disc; the court always serves another. The range runs on a four-minute clock
Saved games
A running program saves itself every few seconds and whenever you pause or leave. RESUME on the table picks it up, kit and all. Finished runs go in the record: best score per program, on the arch
The wardrobe
The console behind the table: point at a button and pull the trigger — gender, species, affiliation, trousers or skirt. The mannequin beside it wears your choices; so do you, down to the boots
The line
Empty hand, squeeze grip with nothing in reach: a hook fires from the wrist bracer. Cast it toward a lost weapon and it homes, latches and reels it back into your hand. Cast at nothing and it is a rope dart — swing it and the hook hits what a blade would. Let go of grip to reel in (desktop: T)
