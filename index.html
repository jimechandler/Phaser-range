Holodeck · Recreational Program

Phaser Range

A circular stage in a dark void. Light-targets drift through the black — pop them. A training remote orbits you: when its emitter glows and chimes, a stun bolt follows — step aside or duck, or shoot the bolt out of the air. The instant it fires, the remote flares white: tag it then for the big score. You can hit it any time to make it flinch — counter-fire during the glow breaks its attack. Each hand cycles its own rack — the First Contact pistol, the swept TNG type 2 you hold flat like a remote, a bat'leth that bats its bolts straight back, and empty hands if you want them. Three hits ends the program.

Voyager's court, walls lit in holodeck gold. An eight-inch disc runs the walls, ricocheting off every surface — and when it has run them long enough, it comes looking for you. Shoot it to send it back where your muzzle points, and it returns faster every time. Take it in the head or the chest and that is a full impact: one of your three pips. There is no remote here and nothing else to shoot — just you, the disc, and the walls.
