A circular stage in a dark void. Light-targets drift through the black — pop them. A training remote orbits you: when its emitter glows and chimes, a stun bolt follows — step aside or duck, or shoot the bolt out of the air. The instant it fires, the remote flares white: tag it then for the big score. You can hit it any time to make it flinch — counter-fire during the glow breaks its attack. The armory is laid out on the table: Starfleet phasers and rifles, a bat'leth that bats its bolts straight back, a d'k tahg, the Klingon disruptor that takes targets apart instead of popping them, and a grappling hook that hauls in whatever it catches so you can throw it. Three hits ends the program.

Voyager's court, walls lit in holodeck gold. An eight-inch disc runs the walls, ricocheting off every surface — and when it has run them long enough, it comes looking for you. Shoot it and it ricochets off your beam — angle in, angle out — and comes back faster every time. Take it in the head or the chest and that is a full impact: one of your three pips. There is no remote here and nothing else to shoot — just you, the disc, and the walls.

Hands

Your primary starts in this hand; the rest of the kit rides your hips and back. Rearrange it on the table before each drill.

Hold, then release
The trigger charges the phaser out of its cell; let go and every stored joule flies as one bolt. Snap shots get aim assist — dial it in Settings
The power dial
How long it keeps charging: half a second at stun, about a second mid-dial, and at kill it drinks until the cell is dry — one enormous shot
Light-targets
They hold a charge, shrink as you burn it off, and bolt when your muzzle crosses them
Velocity
A bolt bounces the disc and dumps its charge into it — a full cell sends it screaming. Overfeed one and it divides. Take it in the head or chest and it costs a pip
Keep holding
Stay on past a full charge and it vents as a sustained beam instead — no assist, just its own width
Thumb key
B / Y fires as well — the way the swept TNG shell is meant to be worked
A / X or grip
Tap to cycle that hand's weapons — hold A / X 0.6 s to pause (a fresh press on the pause card ends the program)
Thumbstick
Flick right to raise the power setting, left to lower it — ten detents, stun to kill, the lamp runs green to red (desktop: mouse wheel)
Your body
Dodge stun bolts physically — sidestep, duck
Bat'leth
Always on the table. Either hand, or both for a two-handed grip. Deflects stun bolts even held still; a returned bolt that hits the remote pays 200, and a swing that lands takes a chunk of its hull
Throw a blade
Grip while swinging hard and it flies and sticks where it lands — grip near it again to pull it free (desktop: G throws, F recovers)
Disruptor
Klingon sidearm, two settings, both high: thumbstick left is BOLT (charge and release, never a beam), right is BEAM (the trigger is the beam). Its bolts hit four times as hard as a phaser's, and whatever it burns down disintegrates
The remote's hull
The training remote takes damage now — bolts, beams, blades, a thrown target, a hook slam. Burn its ten cells down and it is destroyed (+300); a quicker one arrives three seconds later. After it fires it sometimes dives in close — that is your bat'leth's moment
Your grip
A blade snaps to your hand but keeps the way you caught it: take the d'k tahg upside down and you hold it reversed; take the bat'leth edge-in and it stays edge-in
Grappling hook
Trigger fires the hook; it latches a light-target, the remote, or the disc and reels it in to arm's length. Swing and release the trigger to throw it — into the arena shell for damage, or through another target for a double. Release still and it lets go; trigger again mid-flight recalls an empty hook
Bolts
A bolt never just vanishes — wherever it ends it goes with a poof in its own colour
Desktop
Mouse look · click / hold fire · WASD roams the whole room · Shift sprint · Space dash (dodge!) · C duck · wheel sets power · B or 1–8 weapon · G throws (a blade, or whatever the hook holds) · P pause
