Holodeck · Recreational Program

Phaser Range

A circular stage in a dark void. Light-targets drift through the black — pop them. A training remote orbits you: when its emitter glows and chimes, a stun bolt follows — step aside or duck, or shoot the bolt out of the air. The instant it fires, the remote flares white: tag it then for the big score. You can hit it any time to make it flinch — counter-fire during the glow breaks its attack. Every hand carries three weapons: the First Contact pistol, the swept TNG type 2 you hold flat like a remote, and a bat'leth that bats its bolts straight back. Three hits ends the program.
